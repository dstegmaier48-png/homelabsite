## 2. Provision and hosting of the website

This website is provided as a static website through GitHub Pages. The hosting service is provided by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States.

When this website is accessed, GitHub processes connection data that is technically required. This may include in particular:

- the IP address of the accessing device,
- the date and time of access,
- the requested page or file,
- the amount of data transferred,
- the browser type and version,
- the operating system,
- the referrer URL,
- information about the device used, and
- the status of the page request.

This processing is necessary to deliver the website and to ensure its stability, functionality and security.

The legal basis for this processing is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest lies in providing this website securely, reliably and without technical errors.

GitHub may also process data in the United States or other countries outside the European Union or European Economic Area. According to GitHub, such transfers rely on the applicable data-protection transfer mechanisms.

Further information about GitHub's processing of personal data is available from:

- [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
- [GitHub Pages data collection information](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection)

I have no control over the specific retention period for server and connection data processed by GitHub. Retention is governed by GitHub's policies and operational requirements.

## 3. Contact by email

An email address is provided on this website for contacting me. No contact form is used.

If you contact me by email, the personal data you provide will be processed. This regularly includes:

- your email address,
- your name, if provided,
- the content of your message, and
- any other information you provide voluntarily.

The data is processed to handle and respond to your enquiry.

If your enquiry relates to initiating or performing a contract, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. In all other cases, processing is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest lies in appropriately handling incoming enquiries and communicating with prospective customers, clients and other contacts.

The data will be deleted as soon as it is no longer required to handle the enquiry and no statutory retention obligations or other legitimate grounds require further storage.

Please note that the confidentiality of an unencrypted email cannot be fully guaranteed during transmission over the internet.

## 4. Cookies and local storage

The controller does not use analytics, marketing or tracking services on this website. No non-essential cookies are set, and no comparable tracking information is stored on or read from visitors' devices by the controller.

Consequently, consent is not currently requested through a cookie banner.

If services or technologies requiring consent are added in the future, this privacy policy and the consent procedure will be updated accordingly.

## 5. No visitor analytics

The controller does not analyse visitor behaviour. In particular, no services such as Google Analytics, Matomo, Plausible Analytics or comparable analytics tools are used.

The controller does not carry out automated decision-making or profiling.

## 8. Retention period

Personal data is stored only for as long as necessary for the respective processing purpose.

Data may be stored for longer where statutory retention periods apply, where the data is required to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, or where another statutory legal basis permits continued storage.

The data will be deleted once the processing purpose no longer applies and the relevant retention periods have expired.

## 10. Right to object

Where personal data is processed on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, Article 21 GDPR gives you the right to object to that processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

Following an objection, the personal data concerned will no longer be processed unless compelling legitimate grounds for the processing can be demonstrated that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
